# The Brewmier League Official Rulebook

*Season 1 — Ratified by the League Committee*

---

## Rule 1 — Real Time Only

A pint must be logged **immediately before drinking** — maximum 2 minutes before the first sip.

Logging a pint retrospectively is a violation. The server timestamp is the only timestamp that counts.

## Rule 2 — No Backdating

All timestamps are server-generated at the moment of submission. No entry can be edited or deleted after submission.

If your phone dies before you log, that pint does not count. Tough but fair.

## Rule 3 — One League, One Group

Each join code belongs to one group of friends. Do not share your code with opposing groups.

## Rule 4 — Seasons

A season runs from its published start date to its published end date. Results are archived at season end.

The league admin sets season dates. Current season standings and all-time records are both visible.

## Rule 5 — Disputes

All disputes go to the league admin. The admin's decision is final.

No moaning in the group chat. Settle it on the table.
